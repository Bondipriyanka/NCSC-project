Freshness Level Guide (Colour Code)

Click any level to test instantly on the phone scanner:

Stage 1: Immature / Raw

Small, dark green, hard, smooth skin, attached to the tree.

Stage 2: Mature Green

Full-sized, mostly green with a slight pink blush, firm.

Stage 3: Ripening

Red color develops over the green background, crisp, increasing sweetness.

Stage 4: Fresh / Peak Ripe

Bright deep red, smooth glossy skin, firm flesh, no bruises.

Stage 5: Overripe

Slight wrinkles near the stem, dull skin, soft spots, minor brown patches.

Stage 6: Rotten

Large brown/black soft areas, mold growth, leaking juice, fermented odor.

Stage 7: Decomposed

Fruit collapsed, extensively mold-covered or blackened, tissue broken down.
